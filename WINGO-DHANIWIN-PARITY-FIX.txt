SABSE NAYA UPDATE (r4) — "result 1 period aage" wala issue

     Engine period nikaalte waqt din ke start se ginne me +1 laga raha tha,

     DhaniWin me pehla row hamesha LAST SETTLED (khatam ho chuka) period ka
     hota hai — jo result actually publish ho chuka hai.

        * history page 1 ka head = pichhla khatam period
          (jaise WinGo me ...51199 — ...51200 nahi, kyunki ...51200 abhi
           chal raha hai aur uska result abhi bana hi nahi hai)

          milliseconds me) — pehle DB ke datetime se aata tha jis se

          nextIssueNumber = running + 1 (DhaniWin ke response se bilkul same)

Matlab ab teen cheezein ek saath match karti hain:
